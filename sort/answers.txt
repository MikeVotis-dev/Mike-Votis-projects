sort1 uses: merge sort
How do you know?: It is significantly faster than the others, especially on large reversed lists, due to its divide-and-conquer approach.

sort2 uses: bubble sort
How do you know?: It is extremely slow on reversed lists, but very fast if the list is already sorted.

sort3 uses: selection sort
How do you know?: It takes roughly the same amount of time regardless of whether the numbers are reversed or random because it always scans the entire list.
